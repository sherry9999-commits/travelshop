> asset yet, and nothing here was generated by AI.

## Kept intentionally without imagery

**Reviews.** No longer mounted. The section renders verified customer reviews
only, and the prototype's invented testimonials have been removed from the
dictionaries rather than shipped behind a disclosure label. The editorial proof
composition is preserved in `src/components/Reviews.jsx` behind a `reviews` prop,
ready for real content. It held no imagery by design — it is a pull quote and an
index of what travellers talk about — so no image slot was ever filled there.

The trip **board** rows under Jordan Trips are also text-only by design: they are
a live-inventory list, not a gallery.
